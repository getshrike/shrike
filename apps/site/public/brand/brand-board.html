The brief

Shrike deletes the code that agents and PRs leave behind, then proves the app still builds. The identity had to work in a terminal, a README, a browser tab and a 16 px favicon — in one colour, without illustration.

exactquietstructuralaccountable

Success: a mark that survives 16 px, inverts as a fill change, and reads as deletion with proof — not as a bird, a shield or a stock dev-tool glyph.

The Break

Two strokes on one lean. The left runs whole; the right breaks at its midpoint. One path survives, the other is accounted for.

  • A measured break, not an accident: the gap is centred and both halves are equal
  • Built on a 2:5 rise with a 36-unit stroke and a 114-unit column offset
  • Three shapes and one fill, so the mark inverts without a redraw
  • Reads at 24 px and holds at 16 px in the tight favicon crop

Notes

The Break is the shipped direction. Ink #0A0A0A and paper #F7F5EF; the mark is three shapes on a 256 grid, the wordmark is Inter Medium with a 16-unit square i-dot turned to the same 2:5 lean.

  • Use the supplied SVG masters; never retype the wordmark
  • Keep 1X clear space, where X is one bar width
  • Run a trademark search before broad public use
